Try:

A structural engineer's way of thinking, laid out where you can follow it.

Say it in your own words

Write "G+4 hostel, 30 by 20 m, Visakhapatnam" and Sthira picks out the storeys, size, place and soil, then tells you what it still needs from you.

Watch the weight travel

See load move from roof to slab, beam, column and foundation. Columns turn amber or red as they get closer to their limit.

Open every number

Each result shows its working: the load, the formula, the unit and what was assumed. Nothing arrives as a bare answer.

Ask "what if?"

Change a slab thickness, concrete grade or soil value and see concrete, steel and footing size shift against your saved baseline.

Take it with you

Get a plain-text report listing your inputs, assumptions, results and limits, ready to copy or print as a PDF.

If you are studying

See why a beam needs steel where it does and how load reaches the ground. The working is always one tap away.

If you run a project

Keep inputs, assumptions and results in one place, with a log of every change.

If you are the client

Understand what your engineer is checking and why the soil report matters.

What Sthira will not do

It will not tell you a building is safe to build. It will not guess soil data, invent code clauses, or hide an assumption or a failed check.

Every result is marked PASS, FAIL, WARNING, INCOMPLETE or NOT VERIFIED. Use it to learn, plan and prepare better questions, then take the design to a licensed engineer.
